About me

I'm a spectroscopy-trained analytical chemist and materials scientist with a PhD in Nanoscience from the University of Copenhagen. Over the past several years I've moved between academic research on luminescent nanomaterials and industry roles applying that expertise to process optimization, method development, and — most recently — analytics development within FOSS's Business Innovation department.

I bring practical experience in chemical processes and spectroscopy to problems in method development, formulation, and quality — aiming for more sustainable processes, better products, and safer solutions in life science and food science. Originally from China, I've built my career and life in Denmark since 2021, including learning Danish along the way.
