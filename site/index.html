What I do

I'm a Marie-Curie trained chemist with a PhD in Nanoscience from the University of Copenhagen, now working as a Senior Scientist at FOSS since January 2026, bridging analytics development and business development. My background spans fluorescence and phosphorescence spectroscopy, supramolecular and carbon-based nanomaterials, and the analytical methods used to characterize and validate them — from academic bench work to industrial process optimization.
